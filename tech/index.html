16%

Employee attrition

Predicting who is likely to leave, using the IBM HR dataset. Only 16% of employees left.

  • Always predicting "stays" is about 84% accurate and finds no leavers, so I judged models on leaver recall and ROC-AUC, not accuracy.
  • Class weighting roughly doubled the leavers a logistic model found (recall 0.30 to 0.60) and gave the best cross-validated ROC-AUC (0.83), at the cost of accuracy.

House price prediction

Linear Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost scored on a held-out test set with 5-fold cross-validation. I corrected a Random Forest score that had been calculated on the training data (test R² 0.990).
