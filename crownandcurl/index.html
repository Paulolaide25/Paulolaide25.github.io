    Aftercare

    Looking after your hair

    Three habits that keep a good wig looking new for years.

    Wash gently

    Use a sulphate-free shampoo every 8 to 10 wears. Rinse with cool water and pat dry.

    Detangle smart

    Start at the ends and work up with a wide-tooth comb while the hair is damp and conditioned.

    Store well

    Keep it on a stand or in a breathable bag, away from heat and direct sunlight.
