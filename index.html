MSc · Data analysis

Why employees leave

30.5% vs 10.4%

Attrition among staff working overtime against those who don't, in the IBM HR data. The youngest group (18 to 25) left at 35.8%, and Sales Representatives at about 40%.

IBM HR dataset, 1,470 employees. Charts built in Python from the public data.
MSc · Machine learning

Accuracy was misleading

84% by guessing

Only 16% of employees left, so always predicting "stays" is about 84% accurate and finds no leavers. I judged models on leaver recall and ROC-AUC. Class weighting roughly doubled the share of leavers a logistic model found (recall 0.30 to 0.60) and gave the best cross-validated ROC-AUC (0.83), at the cost of accuracy.

Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Decision Tree, stratified split, 5-fold cross-validation.
MSc · Regression

House price prediction

Linear Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost scored on a held-out test set and with 5-fold cross-validation. The near-perfect linear fit suggests the data is synthetic, and I corrected an earlier Random Forest score that had been calculated on the training data (test R² 0.990).
