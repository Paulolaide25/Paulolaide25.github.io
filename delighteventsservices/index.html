Allergen information

Eat with confidence

We label every dish and keep an allergen list for each menu. These are the ingredients our kitchen commonly handles:

Peanuts (groundnut)Tree nutsEggsMilkFishCrayfish & shellfishSnailWheat (gluten)SoyaSesameEgusi (melon seed)Iru (locust bean)
