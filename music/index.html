The artist

Dizl is the stage name of Olaide Aroyewun, a singer-songwriter with roots in Lagos, Nigeria and Praslin, Seychelles, now based in the UK. He began singing in 2019 and has released music since 2022, blending Afrobeats rhythm with pop melody and personal storytelling.

His songs lean on resilience, love and feel-good energy. Reviewers have pointed to his vocal ease, restrained production and the emotional honesty of the writing.

He releases independently and works with industry partners on promotion and broadcast, including a TRACE MINT performance.
